# Imidillo report, August 2026

I looked over the sensor data for 1 to 31 August 2026 and compared it with July 2026. Most streams were usable, but several parking, charging and traffic sensors were too patchy to include, and both particulate streams had to stay out because their readings were too stale. The numbers show what changed in the readings, but not why.

## Highlights

- **City Tunnel** exceeded the WHO daily NO2 guideline of 25 µg/m³ on 17 days. The worst daily mean was 54.1 µg/m³.
- **West** exceeded the WHO 8-hour O3 guideline of 100 µg/m³ on 15 days. The worst 8-hour mean was 151.1 µg/m³.
- **West** also exceeded the EU 8-hour O3 target of 120 µg/m³ on 7 days.
- **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was fuller than in July by 13.9 percentage points at equal hour and weekday. Its mean occupancy was 37.9% after 24.2%.
- **Am Blauen Bock 3** was less busy than in July by 22.1 percentage points at equal hour and weekday. Its mean occupancy was 20.0% after 42.6%.

## Parking

I could use 4 of 9 parking lots.

Across the usable lots, a typical weekday was emptiest at 03:00 with 25.4% occupancy and fullest at 12:00 with 43.5%. The weekend effect was not the same everywhere. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was fuller on weekends by 7.8 percentage points, while **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** was emptier by 13.7 percentage points and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** was emptier by 15.6 percentage points. **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** showed no measurable weekend change.

Compared with July, only **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was fuller, by 13.9 percentage points at equal hour and weekday. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** showed no measurable change.

## EV charging

I could use 15 of 18 charging stations.

Across those stations, a typical weekday was quietest at 04:00 with 8.3% charging point occupancy and busiest at 11:00 with 30.9%. The weekend effect differed between stations: some were busier on weekends, some were less busy, and several showed no measurable change.

Among the most notable stations, **Breiter Weg 203** was busier than in July by 17.9 percentage points at equal hour and weekday. Its mean occupancy was 44.6% after 25.6%, and no charging point was free for 31.0% of known time. **Am Blauen Bock 3** was less busy than in July by 22.1 percentage points, with a mean of 20.0% after 42.6%; no charging point was free for 7.2% of known time. **Hegelstraße 4** was also one of the busier sites, with no charging point free for 23.4% of known time, and it was less busy on weekends by 10.2 percentage points.

Across the 13 stations that were comparable with July, only **Breiter Weg 203** was busier and only **Am Blauen Bock 3** was less busy. The other comparable stations showed no measurable change.

## Traffic

I could use 14 of 29 traffic sensors.

Across the usable streets, a typical weekday was fastest at 05:00 with 48.2 km/h and slowest at 16:00 with 34.5 km/h. The weekend effect was not the same across streets: some were faster on weekends, while others showed no measurable change.

Compared with July, none of the comparable streets were faster. Speeds were slower on six streets. The most notable were **Walther Rathenau Strasse**, slower by 1.7 km/h at equal hour and weekday, with a raw mean of 37.8 km/h after 39.3 km/h; **Wittenberger Strasse**, slower by 1.9 km/h; and **Gustav Adolf Strasse**, slower by 1.6 km/h. The other comparable streets showed no measurable change.

## Air quality

For NO2, all 4 stations were usable. Across them, a typical weekday peaked at 09:00 with 25.5 µg/m³ and was lowest at 04:00 with 10.8 µg/m³. The weekend effect differed by station: **City Tunnel** was cleaner on weekends by 9.3 µg/m³ and **Guericke Strasse** was cleaner by 4.8 µg/m³, while **Schleinufer** and **West** showed no measurable weekend change.

No NO2 station exceeded the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³. The WHO daily guideline of 25 µg/m³ was exceeded on 17 days at **City Tunnel**, on 3 days at **Guericke Strasse**, on 3 days at **Schleinufer**, and not at **West**. Compared with July, NO2 was more polluted at **City Tunnel** by 6.8 µg/m³, at **Guericke Strasse** by 4.7 µg/m³, and at **West** by 1.8 µg/m³. **Schleinufer** showed no measurable change.

For O3, **West** was usable. Its typical day was lowest at 08:00 with 46.6 µg/m³ and highest at 18:00 with 102.1 µg/m³. There was no measurable weekend change. Health limits were exceeded: the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ on 15 days and the EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ on 7 days, with a worst 8-hour mean of 151.1 µg/m³. Compared with July, there was no measurable change, even though the raw mean was 73.4 µg/m³ after 69.1 µg/m³.

For PM10, no station was usable because the readings were too stale: unchanged from the previous hour in 61% of hours at **Guericke Strasse**, 59% at **Schleinufer**, and 71% at **West**.

For PM2.5, no station was usable for the same reason: unchanged from the previous hour in 72% of hours at **Guericke Strasse**, 70% at **Schleinufer**, and 78% at **West**.

## Data quality

- Parking: I used 4 of 9 lots. I excluded **Allee-Center** at 48.9% coverage, **City-Carre** at 7.0%, and **Domviertel** at 7.8%. **Hotel-Ratswaage** and **Universitaetsplatz** were silent all month. The longest gaps among usable lots were 54 to 56 hours. At **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, observed free spots reached 114 although total spots were listed as 90, so I used 114 as capacity.
- EV charging: I used 15 of 18 stations. I excluded **AnderBuckauerFaehre1** at 53.9% known hours, **Bethanienstrasse1** at 57.4%, and **Breitscheidstrasse2** at 57.7%. No station was silent all month. The longest gaps were 99 hours at **Gareisstraße 12**, 93 hours at **Hegelstraße 4**, and 84 hours at **Gustav-Adolf-Straße 31**.
- Traffic: I used 14 of 29 streets. The other 15 were excluded because coverage was below 60%, ranging from 4.0% at **Hohepfortewall** to 56.3% at **JohannGottlobNathusiusRing**. No street was silent all month. The longest gap on a usable street was 11 hours at **Erzbergerstrasse**. I also dropped implausible readings above 150 km/h at several streets, and I ignored zero readings at **Askanischer Platz**, **Jerusalembruecken**, **Sandtorstrasse**, and **Wittenberger Strasse**.
- Air quality: NO2 and O3 coverage was 90.5% to 94.5%, with gaps of 3 to 5 hours. PM10 and PM2.5 were excluded everywhere because too many hourly values repeated without change.
