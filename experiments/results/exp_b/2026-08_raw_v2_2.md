# Imidillo report, August 2026

I looked through August and compared it with July. The month gave me a mixed picture: I had enough data for some parts of the network, but not for all of it, and the clearest air-quality signal was ozone rather than traffic-related pollution.

## Highlights

- I could use 4 of the 9 parking lots and all 18 EV charging stations.
- On weekdays, the usable parking lots were quietest at 03:00 and busiest at 11:00.
- I saw no **NO2** hourly exceedance of the 200 µg/m³ EU limit. The highest **NO2** hour was 98.0 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** at 21:00 on 14 August.
- **O3 West** exceeded the WHO 8-hour guideline on 9 days and the EU 8-hour target on 5 days. In July, those counts were 5 and 0.
- I could not use any **PM10** or **PM2.5** sensor, because those readings changed too little from one hour to the next.

## Parking

I could use **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim**, and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**.

On weekdays, these four lots together were quietest at 03:00 and busiest at 11:00. The clearest weekday pattern was at **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**, which was often between 74.0% and 87.2% in the late morning and around midday, while **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** stayed very low overnight, often between 2.2% and 11.4%.

Weekend and weekday use were clearly different, but not in the same way everywhere. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was fuller at weekends, especially later in the day. It reached 86.7% on 8 August at 22:00, 100.0% on 15 August at 21:00, and 88.1% on 30 August at 19:00. On many weekdays at the same part of the day it stayed much lower. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** showed the opposite pattern, with fuller weekday middays than weekend middays.

Compared with July, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was fuller overall in August, while **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was emptier overall. At **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**, I did not see a clear month-to-month change.

## EV charging

I used 18 stations.

The weekday charging pattern was broad rather than sharp. Use was usually quietest in the small hours, built up after 08:00, stayed busiest from late morning into early evening, and then eased again after 20:00. A few stations stayed busy for long stretches, especially **Breiter Weg 203**, **Hegelstraße 4**, and **Julius-Bremer-Straße 1**, which often sat at 50.0% to 100.0% for several hours in a row.

I did not see one clear weekend-weekday split across the whole charging network. Some stations were busier on weekend afternoons, while others stayed steadier on weekdays. Compared with July, I did not see a clear citywide change in charging occupancy.

## Air quality

I could use all 4 **NO2** sites and **O3 West**.

At the traffic-oriented **NO2** sites, weekdays were usually more polluted than weekends through most daytime hours. The clearest pattern was at **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse**, and **Schleinufer**, where values rose after 07:00 and often again from late afternoon into the evening. **West** stayed much lower than those three sites through the month.

I saw no **NO2** hourly breach of the EU limit of 200 µg/m³. Daily means still went above the WHO 25 µg/m³ guideline on some days at **City Tunnel**, **Schleinufer**, and **Guericke Strasse**, but not at **West**.

For ozone, the pattern was the reverse. **O3 West** was usually highest in the afternoon and evening, and weekend afternoons were often higher than weekday afternoons. The highest hourly value was 173.0 µg/m³ at 17:00 on 15 August. The 8-hour ozone value went above the WHO 100 µg/m³ guideline on 9 days and above the EU 120 µg/m³ target on 5 days. That was more than in July, when I saw 5 WHO exceedance days and no EU-target exceedance day.

I did not describe particulate pollution this month, because the particle sensors were not reliable enough.

## Data quality

I could not use these parking lots because they had readings in fewer than 60% of the hours: **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Tiefgarage City Carré**, **Tiefgarage Domviertel**, **Parkhaus Hotel Ratswaage**, and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**.

All 18 EV charging stations had enough data for me to use them.

For air quality, I used **NO2** at **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer**, and **West**, plus **O3 West**. I did not use **PM10** at **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer**, or **West**, and I did not use **PM2.5** at those same three sites, because the values stayed unchanged from one hour to the next in more than half of the hours.
