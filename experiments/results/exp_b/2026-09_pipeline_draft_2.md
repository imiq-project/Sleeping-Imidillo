# Imidillo report, September 2026

I looked over Magdeburg’s full sensor data for September 2026. The clearest signals were repeated NO2 daily-guideline exceedances, a cleaner month for ozone at **West**, and busier EV charging at a few stations. I can only report what the measurements show, not why.

## Highlights

- **City Tunnel** exceeded the WHO daily NO2 guideline of 25 µg/m³ on 11 days; the worst daily mean was 49.8 µg/m³.
- **Schleinufer** also exceeded that NO2 guideline on 11 days; the worst daily mean was 35.0 µg/m³.
- **Guericke Strasse** exceeded the same NO2 guideline on 8 days; the worst daily mean was 38.9 µg/m³.
- **West** was cleaner for O3 than in August by 15.3 µg/m³ at the same hour and weekday, with the raw mean moving from 73.4 to 58.3 µg/m³.
- **Leipziger Straße 45** and **Freiligrathstraße 1** were busier for EV charging than in August by 16.7 and 14.7 percentage points.

## Parking

I had usable parking data from 4 of 9 lots. On a typical weekday, occupancy was lowest at 03:00 at 23.8% and highest at 12:00 at 48.6%.

Weekend behaviour differed by lot. **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was emptier on weekends by 13.5 percentage points, and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** was emptier by 15.0 percentage points. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** and **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** showed no measurable weekend change.

Compared with August at the same hour and weekday, only **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** was fuller, by 4.1 percentage points; its raw mean moved from 26.6% to 31.3%. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** showed no measurable change.

## EV charging

I had usable data from 17 of 18 charging stations. On a typical weekday, charging point occupancy was lowest at 03:00 at 6.7% and highest at 11:00 at 39.8%, then eased through the afternoon and evening.

Weekend behaviour did not look the same across stations, and most stations showed no measurable weekend change. Compared with August at the same hour and weekday, 4 stations were busier, 1 was less busy, and 10 showed no measurable change.

The clearest rises were at **Leipziger Straße 45**, busier by 16.7 percentage points, with the raw mean moving from 9.1% to 25.6%, and **Freiligrathstraße 1**, busier by 14.7 percentage points, with the raw mean moving from 18.5% to 32.6%. **Hegelstraße 4** was the busiest station in September: no point was free for 34.5% of known time, and it was busier than in August by 13.9 percentage points. One station was less busy than in August, while the rest showed no measurable month-on-month change.

## Traffic

I had usable traffic data from 12 of 29 streets. On a typical weekday, average speed was highest at 04:00 at 52.7 km/h and lowest at 14:00 at 32.3 km/h.

Weekend speeds were faster on several streets, but not on every street, and the weekend effect differed between streets.

Compared with August at the same hour and weekday, only **Askanischer Platz** was slower, by 1.7 km/h; its raw mean moved from 39.2 km/h to 37.4 km/h. The other 11 comparable streets showed no measurable change.

## Air quality

For NO2, I had usable data from 4 stations. On a typical weekday, the median profile was lowest at 04:00 at 12.8 µg/m³ and highest at 21:00 at 30.0 µg/m³.

At **Guericke Strasse** and **Schleinufer**, NO2 was lower on weekends by 5.1 and 5.9 µg/m³. **City Tunnel** and **West** showed no measurable weekend change. Compared with August at the same hour and weekday, all 4 NO2 stations showed no measurable change.

No NO2 station exceeded the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³. The WHO daily guideline of 25 µg/m³ was exceeded on 11 days at **City Tunnel**, 11 days at **Schleinufer**, 8 days at **Guericke Strasse**, and 1 day at **West**.

For O3, I had one usable station, **West**. Its typical day was lowest at 08:00 at 36.4 µg/m³ and highest at 18:00 at 86.7 µg/m³. There was no measurable weekend change. Compared with August at the same hour and weekday, O3 was cleaner by 15.3 µg/m³, with the raw mean moving from 73.4 to 58.3 µg/m³. The EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ was not exceeded, but the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ was exceeded on 4 days.

I could not use PM10 this time: **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** and **West** were excluded because values were unchanged from the previous hour in 55%, 52% and 62% of hours.

I could not use PM2.5 either: **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** and **West** were excluded because values were unchanged from the previous hour in 64%, 63% and 70% of hours.

## Data quality

- Parking: 5 of 9 lots were excluded. **Allee-Center**, **City-Carre** and **Domviertel** were below 60% coverage, and **Hotel-Ratswaage** and **Universitaetsplatz** had no data in this window and were silent all month. Each usable parking lot had a longest gap of 69 h. At **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, the observed maximum free-spots value was 114 while total spots was 90, so 114 was used as capacity.
- EV charging: **AnderBuckauerFaehre1** had no data in this window and was silent all month. Among usable stations, the longest gaps were 113 h at **Gareisstraße 12** and 95 h at **Berliner Chaussee 11**.
- Traffic: 17 of 29 streets were excluded because coverage was below 60%. The longest gaps among usable streets reached 70 h at **Schleinufer** and 69 h at **Hohepfortestrasse** and **Markgrafenstrasse**. Implausible readings above 150 km/h were dropped on several streets, with the largest count at **Universitaetsplatz** with 36 dropped readings.
- Air quality: NO2 coverage ranged from 80.4% to 83.5%, with longest gaps of 71 h at **Schleinufer** and 69 h at **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse** and **West**. O3 at **West** had 83.5% coverage and a longest gap of 69 h.
