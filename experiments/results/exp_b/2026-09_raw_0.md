# Imidillo report, September 2026

I looked through September and compared it with August where the same hours were available. The middle of the month has a clear data gap in several networks, so I have stayed close to the sensors that were steady enough to trust.

## Highlights

- Weekday parking was most consistently busy at **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**, with the regular high point around 11:00 and around 80% occupancy.
- **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was much fuller on weekends than on weekdays, reaching 98.6% on 20 September at 11:00.
- I could use 6 of the 18 EV charging stations. Their usual pattern was quiet overnight, busier from late morning into early evening, and softer again after 20:00.
- No NO2 reading exceeded the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³. The highest value was 118 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** on 28 September at 18:00.
- O3 at **West** exceeded the WHO 8-hour guideline on 27 and 30 September, but I did not see an 8-hour value above the EU target.

## Parking

I could use 5 of the 9 parking sensors. Across those lots, the weekday pattern was quietest around 03:00 to 06:00 and fullest around 11:00.

The clearest regular weekday peak was at **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**. At 11:00 it was usually in the high 70s to mid 80s, and late at night it was mostly around 33% to 35%. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** also kept a strong late-morning to early-afternoon peak, while **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** stayed much lower on ordinary weekdays.

The strongest weekday-weekend split was at **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**. At 11:00 on many weekdays it sat between 29.1% and 50.4%, but it reached 97.8% on Saturday 19 September and 98.6% on Sunday 20 September. I did not see a comparably strong weekend lift in the city-centre garages.

Against August, I did not see a clear month-wide shift in the reliable garages at comparable weekday hours. **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** stayed around 80% at 11:00 in both months, and **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** kept the same late-morning peak pattern.

## EV charging

I used 6 of the 18 charging stations.

The general pattern was steady across the month. On weekdays, charging activity was usually lowest overnight, started to rise after 07:00, stayed busiest from late morning into early evening, and eased again after 20:00. Weekends kept a similar broad shape, but the early-morning rise was less sharp.

Among the stations I could use, **Ernst-Reuter-Allee 6**, **Am Blauen Bock 3** and **Julius-Bremer-Straße 1** all reached 100% at times. Compared with August, I did not measure a clear month-on-month shift in the reliable stations. The same late-morning to early-evening plateau was already present in August.

## Air quality

I could use all four NO2 sites and the O3 site at **West**. I could not rely on the PM sensors this month, so I am not describing PM10 or PM2.5 conditions.

NO2 kept a clear weekday rhythm. Values were lowest before dawn, rose through the morning, and usually rose again from late afternoon into the evening. Weekend NO2 was lower than weekday NO2 at the traffic sites. At **City Tunnel**, weekend morning values were mostly 6 to 18 µg/m³, while weekday 08:00 to 09:00 values often reached 24 to 62 µg/m³.

I did not see any NO2 hour above the EU limit of 200 µg/m³. The highest value was 118 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** on 28 September at 18:00. I also saw clear NO2 daily-mean values above the WHO level of 25 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel**, including 29 and 30 September. Compared with August, I did not see a clear network-wide NO2 shift.

At **West**, O3 exceeded the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ on 27 and 30 September. I did not see an 8-hour value above the EU target of 120 µg/m³. Compared with August at the same afternoon hours, ozone was lower. In August, late-afternoon hourly values often went above 120 µg/m³, while in September the highest hourly value was 120 µg/m³ at 18:00 on 28 September.

## Data quality

Most parking and air-quality datasets have a clear gap from 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00. Charging has a gap from 12 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00.

For parking, I could not use **Tiefgarage City Carré** or **Tiefgarage Domviertel** because they had readings in fewer than 60% of the hours. **Parkhaus Hotel Ratswaage** and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** also had too little coverage to describe.

For EV charging, I left out **An der Buckauer Fähre 1** because it had readings in fewer than 60% of the hours. I also left out **Bethanienstraße 1**, **Breiter Weg 203**, **Erzbergerstraße 20**, **Freiligrathstraße 1**, **Gareisstraße 12**, **Gustav-Adolf-Straße 31**, **Halberstädter Strasse 137**, **Hegelstraße 19**, **Hegelstraße 4**, **Leipziger Straße 45** and **Liebknechtstraße 37** because their values changed too little from one hour to the next.

For particles, the PM10 and PM2.5 sensors at **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** and **West** changed too little from hour to hour, so they were not reliable enough to describe.
