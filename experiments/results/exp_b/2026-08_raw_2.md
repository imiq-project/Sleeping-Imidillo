# Imidillo report, August 2026

I had enough good data to describe part of the parking, charging and air picture for August. Some sensors were too patchy or too static, so I left them out and say that plainly below. In air quality, nitrogen dioxide stayed far below the EU hourly limit, while ozone had several strong 8-hour episodes.

## Highlights

- In the parking data I could use, the typical weekday was quietest at 03:00 and fullest at 11:00.
- At 12:00 on weekdays, average occupancy was 77.5% at **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**, 56.0% at **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, 37.3% at **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and 36.6% at **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**.
- Weekend parking split clearly by place: at 12:00, **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** was 33.3 percentage points emptier on weekends than on weekdays, while **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was 20.6 percentage points fuller at 18:00.
- **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was clearly fuller than in July at comparable weekday noon hours: 37.3% in August versus 23.3% in July, up 14.0 percentage points.
- At **West**, ozone exceeded the WHO 8-hour guideline on 10 days in August, up from 4 days in July. It exceeded the EU 8-hour target on 5 days in August after none in July.

## Parking

I could use 4 of the 9 parking lots: **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**.

Their weekday pattern was clear. The quietest point was 03:00, with **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** usually close to 10% and **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** fixed at 4.4% when it reported. Occupancy then built through the morning and was fullest at 11:00. The strongest weekday pressure sat at **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**, which averaged 78.4% at 11:00 and 77.5% at 12:00.

Weekend and weekday use differed by location. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** was much quieter on weekends at noon, averaging 44.2% at 12:00 against 77.5% on weekdays. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** moved the other way later in the day: at 18:00 it averaged 41.3% on weekends and 20.7% on weekdays.

Against July, I saw one clear increase and one lot with no measurable change. **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** rose from a weekday 12:00 average of 23.3% in July to 37.3% in August. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** moved from 74.8% to 77.5% at that same hour, which I do not call a measurable change.

## EV charging

I could use 11 of the 18 charging stations.

The general shape was steady and easy to read. A typical weekday was quietest at 03:00. Use then spread from late morning into the afternoon and evening, with the broadest occupancy around 13:00. Across the stations I could use, 0.0% was common overnight, while 50.0% and 100.0% became common from late morning onward.

I saw that same shape at stations such as **Am Blauen Bock 3**, **Ernst-Reuter-Allee 6** and **Liebknechtstraße 37**. I did not see a measurable overall change from July at comparable weekday hours and days. I also did not see a clear overall weekend-versus-weekday difference across the usable charging stations.

## Air quality

I could use all 4 **NO2** sensors and the **O3** sensor at **West**.

For **NO2**, I did not see any hourly value near the EU limit of 200 µg/m³. The monthly peaks were 98 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** at 21:00 on 14 August, 71 µg/m³ at **Guericke Strasse** at 10:00 on 10 August, 95 µg/m³ at **Schleinufer** at 22:00 on 9 August, and 36 µg/m³ at **West** at 09:00 on 10 August. The WHO daily mean guideline of 25 µg/m³ was exceeded on some days at the busier **NO2** sites, but I did not see any hourly breach of the EU limit.

For **O3** at **West**, August was the stronger month. The highest hourly value was 173 µg/m³ at 17:00 on 15 August. I found WHO 8-hour guideline exceedances on 10 days in August, up from 4 days in July. I found EU 8-hour target exceedances on 5 days in August, after none in July. One clear August episode ran from 13:00 to 20:00 on 14 August, when the hourly values were 122, 138, 142, 144, 148, 153, 154 and 150 µg/m³.

I could not describe particulate pollution this month because I could not use the particulate sensors reliably.
