# Imidillo report, September 2026

I saw a clear mid-month hole in the data, but there was still enough solid information to describe several parking lots, 17 charging stations, and the reliable **NO2** and **O3** sensors. Outside that gap, the main parking and charging patterns looked steady, and the strongest air-quality signal was lower ozone than in August.

## Highlights

- There was a shared gap from 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00 across the networks.
- In parking, **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** was the fullest regular city-centre lot, typically reaching 77.9% to 86.7% on weekdays between 10:00 and 12:00.
- **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** stood out on weekends, reaching 97.8% at 11:00 on 19 September and 98.6% at 11:00 on 20 September.
- I used 17 EV charging stations. The usual pattern was quiet nights and busier hours from late morning into early evening.
- No hourly **NO2** value exceeded 200 µg/m³. **O3** at **West** reached 120 µg/m³ at 18:00 on 28 September, and late-month 8-hour averages went above the WHO 100 µg/m³ guideline but not the EU 120 µg/m³ target.

## Parking

I could use four parking lots: **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim**, and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**.

The two regular city-centre garages had a clear weekday rhythm. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** was quietest around 03:00 to 05:00, usually at 4.4% to 7.0%, then busiest from 10:00 to 12:00, often between 55.3% and 86.8%. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** followed the same shape but at a much fuller level: around 33% to 35% in the early morning, then commonly 77.9% to 86.7% between 10:00 and 12:00.

Weekend and weekday behaviour differed by location. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** was clearly fuller on weekdays than on weekends. Its late-morning Saturday values were mostly around 44.4% to 53.2%, and Sunday values were often around 36.5% to 40.0%, well below weekday late-morning levels. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** showed the opposite pattern. On many weekdays it stayed roughly between 15% and 52% through the day, but weekend midday values were often much higher, including 71.1% at 10:00 and 97.8% at 11:00 on 19 September, and 73.0% at 10:00 and 98.6% at 11:00 on 20 September.

Compared with August, the usable parking lots kept broadly the same daily shape. I did not see a measurable month-to-month shift in the regular weekday pattern.

## EV charging

I used 17 stations.

Across the network, the typical pattern was straightforward. The quietest hours were overnight, especially from 00:00 to 06:00, when many stations sat at 0% or 50%. Use usually built after 07:00 and was busiest from late morning into early evening. That broad shape looked much like August, so I did not see a measurable citywide shift.

A few stations were busy for long stretches. **Hegelstraße 4** was often full at 100%, including 00:00 to 07:00 on 16 September and 00:00 to 06:00 on 28 September. **Breiter Weg 203** frequently reached 100% in daytime, for example from 11:00 to 23:00 on 11 September. **Liebknechtstraße 37** was one of the busiest evening sites and stayed at 100% from 17:00 to 23:00 on 8 September.

Weekdays had the clearest daytime peak. On weekends I still saw busy midday and evening periods at several stations, so weekend charging was not broadly quieter.

## Air quality

I could use all four **NO2** sites and **O3** at **West**. I am not describing particles here because those sensors were not reliable enough this month.

For **NO2**, the quietest hours were usually around 03:00 to 05:00, and the busiest periods were the daytime peaks around 07:00 to 10:00 and again from late afternoon into the evening. The highest hourly value I saw was 118 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** at 18:00 on 28 September. That means the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³ was not breached. Daily mean **NO2** did rise above the WHO 25 µg/m³ guideline on some days at **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse**, and **Schleinufer**. I did not see that at **West**. Compared with August, I did not see a measurable overall shift in **NO2**.

For **O3** at **West**, the pattern was reversed: lower in the morning and highest in the late afternoon and evening. The monthly maximum was 120 µg/m³ at 18:00 on 28 September. I did not see any 8-hour average reach the EU target of 120 µg/m³. I did see 8-hour averages above the WHO 100 µg/m³ guideline late in the month, including the 14:00 to 21:00 periods on 27 and 30 September. Ozone was lower than in August, when many afternoon hours were above 120 µg/m³.

In EV charging, I left out **An der Buckauer Fähre 1** because it reported in fewer than 60% of the hours.

In air quality, I could use all **NO2** sites and **O3** at **West**. I could not use **PM10** at **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer**, or **West**, or **PM2.5** at those same three sites, because the readings stayed unchanged from one hour to the next too often.
