# Imidillo report, September 2026

I saw a clear break in the month: many sensor series disappeared between 11 Sep 12:00 and 14 Sep 08:00. Outside that gap, the city still showed stable enough patterns for me to describe parking, charging and air quality with some confidence. I am only using sensors that were reliable enough this month.

## Highlights

- On weekdays, the usable city-centre parking lots were usually fullest late in the morning, especially around 11:00, and quietest before dawn.
- Weekend parking was clearly lighter in the centre, but **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** often did the opposite and reached 97.8% at 11:00 on 19 Sep and 98.6% at 11:00 on 20 Sep.
- I could use 15 of the 18 EV charging stations. Charging was usually light overnight and fuller from late morning into early evening.
- The highest NO2 reading was 118 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** on 28 Sep at 18:00. I saw no NO2 hourly EU-limit exceedance.
- I saw no O3 EU 8-hour target exceedance. The WHO 8-hour guideline was exceeded at **West** on 27 Sep and 30 Sep.

## Parking

I could use five of the nine parking sensors.

On weekdays, the clearest city-centre pattern was at **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**. Around 11:00 it usually sat between 70.0% and 86.7%, while around 03:00 to 05:00 it stayed near 33.9% to 34.7%. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** showed the same shape, with most weekday 11:00 values between 45.6% and 86.8%, and night values around 4.4% to 8.8%.

Weekends were clearly quieter in the centre. At **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, Sunday 11:00 was 1.3% on 20 Sep and 6.6% on 27 Sep, far below weekday 11:00 levels. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** also dropped on weekends, with weekend late-morning values between 36.5% and 47.9%.

The main exception was **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**. Its weekends were often much fuller than its weekdays, reaching 97.8% at 11:00 on 19 Sep and 98.6% at 11:00 on 20 Sep. On many weekdays, the same lot spent late morning much lower, often in the high 20s to low 50s.

Compared with August at comparable hours and day types, I did not see a clear citywide shift in parking. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** kept the same broad late-morning weekday range, and **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** had strong weekend peaks in both months.

## EV charging

I could use 15 of the 18 charging stations.

The network kept a clear daily rhythm. Overnight use was usually light, it rose after about 07:00 to 08:00, and it was generally fullest from late morning into early evening. Weekends were more mixed from place to place, but nights were still quieter than daytime.

Across the usable network, I did not see a clear month-to-month shift from August. The daytime pattern stayed broadly the same: fuller working-day hours, lighter nights, and scattered 100% hours at individual stations rather than across the whole network at once.

## Air quality

I could use all four NO2 sensors and the O3 sensor at **West**.

For NO2, the highest hourly value was 118 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** on 28 Sep at 18:00. That stayed below the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³. I did see daily means above the WHO 25 µg/m³ guideline on some days at **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse** and **Schleinufer**. I did not see an hourly EU-limit exceedance anywhere.

The weekday NO2 pattern at the busier sites was lowest before dawn and higher from the morning onward, with several of the month’s stronger hours in the evening. Examples were **City Tunnel** at 107 µg/m³ on 28 Sep at 21:00 and 94 µg/m³ on 30 Sep at 20:00.

For O3 at **West**, I did not see any EU 8-hour target exceedance. I did see WHO 8-hour guideline exceedances on 27 Sep and 30 Sep. Compared with August, ozone was clearly less elevated overall: September had no EU 8-hour target exceedance, while August did.

I am not describing PM10 or PM2.5 in the air-quality results because those sensors were not reliable enough this month.

## Data quality

Many series were missing from 11 Sep 12:00 to 14 Sep 08:00. That reduced coverage, but it did not disqualify the reliable NO2, O3, parking and most EV sensors.

For parking, I could not use **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Tiefgarage City Carré**, **Tiefgarage Domviertel** or **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** because they reported in fewer than 60% of the hours.

For EV charging, I could not use **An der Buckauer Fähre 1** or **Gareisstraße 12** because they reported in fewer than 60% of the hours, and I could not use **Freiligrathstraße 1** because its value stayed unchanged from one hour to the next too often.

For particles, I could not use the PM10 or PM2.5 sensors at **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** or **West** because their values changed too little from hour to hour.
