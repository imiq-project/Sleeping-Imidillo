# Imidillo report, September 2026

I looked across Magdeburg’s parking, charging, traffic and air sensors for 1 to 30 September 2026. Most series showed no measurable change versus August, but a few signals stood out clearly, especially in air quality and EV charging.

## Highlights

- **City Tunnel** exceeded the WHO daily NO2 guideline of 25 µg/m³ on 11 days. The worst daily mean was 49.8 µg/m³.
- **Schleinufer** also exceeded the WHO daily NO2 guideline on 11 days. The worst daily mean was 35.0 µg/m³.
- **Guericke Strasse** exceeded the WHO daily NO2 guideline on 8 days. The worst daily mean was 38.9 µg/m³.
- **West** was cleaner for O3 than in August by 15.3 µg/m³ at equal hour and weekday, with the raw mean moving from 73.4 µg/m³ to 58.3 µg/m³.
- In EV charging, **Leipziger Straße 45** was busier than in August by 16.7 percentage points, and **Freiligrathstraße 1** was busier by 14.7 percentage points.

## Parking

I could use 4 of 9 parking lots.

On a typical weekday across the usable lots, occupancy was 23.8% at 03:00 and 48.6% at 12:00. Weekend behavior differed between lots. **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was emptier on weekends by 13.5 percentage points, and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** was emptier by 15.0 percentage points. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** and **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** showed no measurable weekend change.

Compared with August, only **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** was fuller, by 4.1 percentage points at equal hour and weekday, with the raw mean moving from 26.6% to 31.3%. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim**, and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** showed no measurable change.

## EV charging

I could use 17 of 18 charging stations.

The median weekday profile was 6.7% at 03:00 and 39.8% at 11:00, then was 30.6% at 15:00, 31.6% at 16:00, 30.8% at 17:00, and 30.1% at 18:00. Weekend effects differed between stations. Compared with August, 4 comparable stations were busier, 1 was less busy, and the rest showed no measurable change.

The busiest station this period was **Hegelstraße 4**: no point was free 34.5% of the known time, and it was also busier than in August by 13.9 percentage points, with the raw mean moving from 39.0% to 53.9%. **Leipziger Straße 45** was busier by 16.7 percentage points, with the raw mean moving from 9.1% to 25.6%, and had no point free 12.4% of the known time. **Freiligrathstraße 1** was busier by 14.7 percentage points, with the raw mean moving from 18.5% to 32.6%, and had no point free 12.3% of the known time.

## Traffic

I could use 12 of 29 traffic sensors.

Across the usable streets, weekday speeds were 52.7 km/h at 04:00 and 32.3 km/h at 14:00. Weekend effects differed between streets. At several streets, weekend speeds were faster; at others, there was no measurable weekend effect.

Compared with August, only **Askanischer Platz** was slower, by 1.7 km/h at equal hour and weekday, with the raw mean moving from 39.2 km/h to 37.4 km/h. The other 11 comparable streets showed no measurable change.

## Air quality

For NO2, I could use all 4 stations. Across them, the weekday profile was 12.8 µg/m³ at 04:00 and 30.0 µg/m³ at 21:00. The weekend pattern was similar across the NO2 stations. **Guericke Strasse** was cleaner on weekends by 5.1 µg/m³, and **Schleinufer** was cleaner by 5.9 µg/m³; **City Tunnel** and **West** showed no measurable weekend change.

Against health limits, none of the NO2 stations exceeded the EU hourly limit. The WHO daily guideline was exceeded on 11 days at **City Tunnel**, with a worst daily mean of 49.8 µg/m³; on 11 days at **Schleinufer**, with a worst of 35.0 µg/m³; on 8 days at **Guericke Strasse**, with a worst of 38.9 µg/m³; and on 1 day at **West**, with a worst of 26.1 µg/m³. Versus August, all 4 NO2 stations showed no measurable change.

For O3, I could use **West**. On a typical day there, O3 was 36.4 µg/m³ at 08:00 and 86.7 µg/m³ at 18:00. I saw no measurable weekend change. The EU 8-hour target was not exceeded. The WHO 8-hour guideline was exceeded on 4 days, with a worst 8-hour value of 106.6 µg/m³. Compared with August, **West** was cleaner by 15.3 µg/m³ at equal hour and weekday, with the raw mean moving from 73.4 µg/m³ to 58.3 µg/m³.

I could not use any PM10 station: **GuerickeStrasse** was excluded because the value was unchanged from the previous hour in 55% of hours, **Schleinufer** in 52% of hours, and **West** in 62% of hours.

I could not use any PM2.5 station: **GuerickeStrasse** was excluded because the value was unchanged from the previous hour in 64% of hours, **Schleinufer** in 63% of hours, and **West** in 70% of hours.
