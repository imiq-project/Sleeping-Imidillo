# Imidillo report, August 2026

I looked through August hour by hour. The clearest stories this month came from four parking lots, all 18 charging stations, and the reliable **NO2** and **O3** sensors. I could not trust the particulate sensors enough to describe them.

## Highlights
- **NO2** stayed well below the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³ everywhere I could use it. The highest hour was 98 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** at 21:00 on 14 August.
- **O3** at **West** was the main air-quality pressure point. The highest hour was 173 µg/m³ at 17:00 on 15 August, the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ was exceeded on 10 days, and the EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ on 5 days.
- In parking, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** had the clearest weekday rise into late morning and midday, while **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was much fuller on weekends than on weekdays.
- I could use 4 of the 9 parking lots and all 18 EV charging stations.
- I could not use any **PM10** or **PM2.5** sensor because those readings changed too little from hour to hour.

## Parking

I could rely on **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**.

The clearest weekday parking pattern was in **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**. At **Friedensplatz**, the quietest part of a typical weekday was late evening, around 22:00, when it was often in the low single digits to low teens. It then climbed through the morning and was usually busiest from 11:00 to 13:00, often around 50% to 70%. **Ulrichshaus** also built up through the morning and was usually busiest around 11:00 to 13:00, commonly around 75% to 85%, before easing back toward the mid-30s late in the evening.

Weekend and weekday use were clearly different. **Friedensplatz** and **Ulrichshaus** were much emptier on weekends than on weekdays at the same midday hours. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** showed the opposite pattern: many weekday daytime hours sat roughly between 20% and 45%, while weekend peaks reached 94.9% at 12:00 on 08 August and 100.0% at 21:00 on 15 August.

**Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** followed a different shape. It was often fuller late in the evening and overnight than in the middle of the day. Compared with July, it was fuller at many comparable late-evening hours. **Friedensplatz** went the other way and was emptier late in the evening than in July. At **Ulrichshaus**, I did not see a measurable month-to-month change.

## EV charging

I could use all 18 charging stations.

The general pattern was steady but spread out. On weekdays, the quietest period was usually the early morning, around 04:00 to 06:00. Activity then built from late morning into the evening. Weekends leaned a bit more toward afternoon and evening use, while weekdays were more spread across working hours. Compared with July, I did not see a measurable overall change in that pattern.

A few stations stood out for long busy stretches. **Breiter Weg 203** was often at 100.0%. **Hegelstraße 4** also spent long runs at 50.0% or 100.0%. **Julius-Bremer-Straße 1** was frequently very busy as well, including many 100.0% hours from the middle of the month onward.

## Air quality

I could use **NO2** at **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** and **West**, and **O3** at **West**.

For **NO2**, the weekday pattern at the traffic sites was lowest in the early morning and higher from the morning into the evening. Weekend levels were usually lower than weekday levels. I did not see a clear month-wide change from July. The EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³ was never reached. The highest August **NO2** hour was 98 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** at 21:00 on 14 August. The WHO daily-mean guideline of 25 µg/m³ was exceeded on several days at **City Tunnel** and on some days at **Schleinufer**.

For **O3**, the daily shape was the reverse: lowest in the early morning and highest in the late afternoon and early evening. The highest value was 173 µg/m³ at **West** at 17:00 on 15 August. The WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ was exceeded on 10 days: 03, 04, 05, 09, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15 and 27 August. The EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ was exceeded on 5 days: 04, 09, 10, 14 and 15 August. Compared with July, the ozone peak was higher in August: 173 µg/m³ versus 151 µg/m³.

## Data quality

I could not use **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Tiefgarage City Carré**, **Tiefgarage Domviertel**, **Parkhaus Hotel Ratswaage** or **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** because they had readings in fewer than 60% of August hours.

I could use all 18 EV charging stations. Long runs of unchanged values are normal there, so that was not a quality problem.

I could not use **PM10** at **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** or **West**, and I could not use **PM2.5** at the same three sites, because those readings stayed unchanged from one hour to the next in more than half of the hours.
