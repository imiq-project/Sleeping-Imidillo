# Imidillo report, September 2026

I saw one clear limitation this month: from 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00 there was a broad data gap across parking, charging and air sensors. Outside that gap, the main daily rhythms were still visible. September ozone was clearly lower than August, while parking and charging mostly kept their usual shapes.

## Highlights

- On reliable parking lots, weekdays were quietest around 03:00 to 05:00 and fullest around 11:00 to 12:00.
- **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** had the sharpest parking peaks, including 100% at 19:00 on 4 September.
- I could use 10 of 18 EV charging stations. The network was quiet overnight and busiest from late morning into early evening.
- No NO2 hour reached the EU limit of 200 µg/m³. The highest value was 118 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** on 28 September at 18:00.
- I did not see any 8-hour O3 value above the EU target of 120 µg/m³. I did see the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ exceeded on 27 and 30 September.

## Parking

I could describe four parking lots with confidence: **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**.

The clearest weekday pattern was in **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**. Both were usually emptiest in the early morning, around 03:00 to 05:00, and fullest around 11:00 to 12:00. In **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, early-morning values were often around 4% to 8%, while late-morning weekday values often reached about 58% to 87%. In **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**, weekday late-morning levels were often around 77% to 87%.

Weekends looked different. **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** was clearly less full on weekend late mornings than on weekdays. On Saturdays 11:00 to 14:00 it was mostly around 44% to 50%, and on Sundays at the same hours it was usually around 37% to 40%. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was the exception: it was much more variable on weekends and on a few evenings, with very high peaks on 19 and 20 September and again on 26 September.

**Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** stood apart from the other reliable lots because it stayed fuller overnight, often around 42% to 66% in the first half of the month. After 19 September its values were much lower for several days, often below 30%.

Compared with August at similar hours and on similar weekday and weekend patterns, I did not see a measurable month-wide change in the reliable parking lots. The strongest signal was stability in **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**, and continued high variability at **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**.

## EV charging

I used 10 charging stations.

Across those stations, the typical pattern was plain: charging was lowest overnight, rose after the morning hours, and was busiest from about 09:00 to 18:00. Weekends were less pointed than weekdays, with more use carrying into the afternoon and early evening.

Two stations stood out to me. **Breiter Weg 203** was often very busy and hit 100% many times, including 11:00 on 11 September and 10:00 on 30 September. **Ernst-Reuter-Allee 6** was also regularly busy through the middle of the day and early evening, including repeated 100% readings on 21, 25 and 28 September.

Compared with August, I did not see a measurable network-wide change across the reliable charging stations. The same broad daytime pattern was still there.

## Air quality

For NO2, the usual weekday pattern stayed visible at the traffic sites. Levels were generally lowest around 03:00 to 05:00, then higher in the morning and again from late afternoon into the evening. Weekends were mostly lower.

No NO2 hour reached the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³. The highest September NO2 value was 118 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** on 28 September at 18:00. The WHO daily-mean guideline of 25 µg/m³ was exceeded on 12 days at **City Tunnel**. I also saw daily-mean exceedances at **Guericke Strasse** and **Schleinufer**. **West** stayed below that daily guideline.

Ozone at **West** stayed below the EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ throughout September. I did, however, see the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ exceeded on 27 and 30 September. Compared with August, ozone was clearly lower: August had several 8-hour periods above 120 µg/m³, while September had none.

I could not give a reliable PM10 or PM2.5 assessment this month, so I am not making any statement about PM guideline exceedances.

## Data quality

There was a shared data gap from 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00.

For parking, I could not use **Tiefgarage Allee Center** or **Tiefgarage City Carré** because they reported in fewer than 60% of hours. I could not use **Tiefgarage Domviertel** because it had too few readings and too many unchanged values. **Parkhaus Hotel Ratswaage** and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** did not have enough September data to describe.

For EV charging, I used 10 of 18 stations. The other 8 had too many gaps or too many unchanged hours. **An der Buckauer Fähre 1** had no September readings.

For air quality, I could use all four NO2 sensors and the O3 sensor at **West**. I could not use PM10 or PM2.5 at **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** or **West** because those values stayed unchanged from one hour to the next too often.
