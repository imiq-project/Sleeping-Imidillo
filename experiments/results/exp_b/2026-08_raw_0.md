# Imidillo report, August 2026

I looked through August hour by hour, and I compared it with July at the same kinds of times. Most of the usable sensors followed steady patterns, with a few clear exceptions in ozone and in some parking sites.

## Highlights

- At **West**, ozone 8-hour averages went above the WHO guideline of 100 on 10 days in August, and above the EU target of 120 on 5 days. In July, those counts were 4 days and 0 days.
- In parking, **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** was clearly a weekday site: around 11:00 to 14:00 it was often 74.0% to 87.2% on weekdays, while on Sundays 9, 16, 23 and 30 August it sat between 37.4% and 40.6% at 13:00.
- **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was the opposite. It was often much fuller on weekends, reaching 94.9% on 8 August 12:00, 100.0% on 15 August 21:00, and 88.1% on 30 August 19:00.
- I saw a clear month-to-month increase at **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** at night. At 00:00 it was 45.5%, 54.9% and 50.9% on 18, 19 and 20 August, against 13.0%, 25.9% and 25.8% on 20, 21 and 22 July.
- I did not see any hourly NO2 value above 200, and I did not see any PM10 or PM2.5 day at or above their daily guideline levels.

## Parking

I could use five parking sensors this month: **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**.

On weekdays, the clearest shared parking pattern was a quiet start and a late-morning to midday rise. **Tiefgarage Allee Center** was usually quietest around 07:00, often near 4.5% to 5.2%, and it was usually fullest around 11:00 to 12:00, such as 40.6% on 11 August 11:00 and 44.7% on 11 August 12:00. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** showed the same timing: it was typically lower around 07:00, then peaked around 11:00 or 12:00, reaching 80.0% on 10 August 12:00, 79.9% on 11 August 11:00 and 87.2% on 31 August 12:00.

The weekday-weekend split was strongest at **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** and **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**. **Universitätsplatz** was clearly less full on weekends, especially on Sundays. By contrast, **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** had several very full weekend afternoons and evenings, while many weekday afternoons stayed much lower, often in the 20% to 50% range.

Against July, I saw no measurable daytime change at **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** or **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**. The clear exception was **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim**, which was fuller in August, especially in the evening and overnight hours.

## EV charging

I used 7 of the 18 charging stations.

Across those usable stations, the usual pattern was quiet overnight, then fuller from late morning into early evening. Many hours sat at 50%, which means one of the two charging points was in use, and there were also regular full hours at 100%.

A few stations showed that pattern very clearly. **Am Blauen Bock 3** reached 100.0% on 13 August from 13:00 to 15:00 and again on 22 August at 14:00 and 15:00. **Ernst-Reuter-Allee 6** reached 99.2% to 100.0% on 20 August from 21:00 to 22:00 and on 21 August from 15:00 to 16:00. **Liebknechtstraße 37** was also often busy in the afternoon and evening, including 100.0% from 18:00 to 21:00 on 20 August.

I did not see a measurable citywide difference between weekdays and weekends across the reliable charging stations. I also did not see a measurable month-to-month change versus July across those same reliable stations.

## Air quality

All air-quality sensors were usable.

For NO2, the road-facing sites usually had their lower values before 06:00 and higher values later in the morning and again in the evening. At **City Tunnel**, weekday morning and daytime values often reached the 30s and 40s, and the month’s hourly maximum there was 98.0 at 21:00 on 14 August. No NO2 sensor came close to the EU hourly limit of 200. The WHO daily-mean NO2 guideline of 25 was exceeded on several days at **City Tunnel**.

Ozone at **West** followed the opposite daily shape: lower in the morning, higher in the late afternoon and evening. The highest hourly value was 173.0 at 17:00 on 15 August. The 8-hour ozone average exceeded the WHO guideline on 10 days and the EU target on 5 days. That was clearly more than in July, when I counted 4 WHO exceedance days and 0 EU-target exceedance days.

For particles, August stayed moderate. PM10 hourly values did not go above 29.0 at **Guericke Strasse**, 33.0 at **Schleinufer** or 23.0 at **West**, and I did not see any PM10 day at or above 45 or 50. I also did not see any PM2.5 day at or above 15.
