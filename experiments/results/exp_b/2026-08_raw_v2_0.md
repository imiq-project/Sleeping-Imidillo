# Imidillo report, August 2026

I looked through August carefully and the month was mixed. Parking and charging gave me a usable picture in most places, while for air quality I could rely on NO2 and ozone, but not on the particle sensors.

## Highlights

- **City Tunnel** had the highest NO2 value I saw this month: 98 µg/m³ at 21:00 on 14 August. No NO2 sensor reached the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³.
- At **West**, ozone exceeded the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ on 10 days and the EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ on 5 days. In July, those counts were 5 days and 0 days.
- **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was the clearest weekend parking hotspot, reaching 94.9% at 12:00 on 8 August and 100.0% at 21:00 on 15 August.
- **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was fuller than in July, especially overnight and in the early morning.
- I could use all 18 EV charging stations, but I could not use any August **PM10** or **PM2.5** sensor because the readings stayed unchanged too often from one hour to the next.

## Parking

I could use **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**.

The weekday parking pattern was quietest in the small hours, usually around 03:00 to 05:00, and busiest from late morning into early afternoon, most often around 11:00 to 13:00. **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** showed that most clearly: on weekdays it often reached 77.4% to 87.2% around midday, while weekend midday values were more often around 46.2% to 52.8%.

Weekend and weekday differences were strongest at **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**. On many weekdays it sat roughly in the 16% to 45% range through the middle of the day, but on weekends it rose much higher, including 94.9% at 12:00 and 98.5% at 17:00 on 8 August, and 100.0% at 21:00 on 15 August.

**Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was steadier than the other usable lots, usually partly full through the whole day and night. Compared with July, it was fuller in August at comparable night and early-morning hours: early July often sat in the mid-teens to low 30s, while August was often around 40% to 55% in those hours. For the other reliable lots, I did not see a clear month-to-month shift.

## EV charging

I could use all 18 charging stations.

The general pattern was clear. Most stations were quietest overnight, then occupancy rose from late morning and stayed busiest from about 11:00 into the evening. Across the month, many stations spent long stretches at 50%, which means one of the two points was in use.

A few stations stayed busy for long runs. **Hegelstraße 4** and **Julius-Bremer-Straße 1** were often at 50% or 100% for hours at a time, and **Breiter Weg 203** was also busy on many days. I did not see a clear citywide weekend-versus-weekday split, and compared with July I did not see a measurable overall change.

## Air quality

For air quality, I could use **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** and **West** for NO2, and **West** for ozone.

NO2 followed a familiar weekday shape. The quietest hours were usually around 04:00 to 06:00, and the busiest were usually around 08:00 to 10:00. Weekend values were generally lower and flatter. The highest August NO2 reading was 98 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** at 21:00 on 14 August. No site reached the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³.

For the WHO daily NO2 guideline of 25 µg/m³, **West** stayed below it through the month. The three traffic sites went above it on some days.

Ozone at **West** showed the opposite daily timing from NO2. It was usually lowest in the morning, around 07:00 to 10:00, and highest from late afternoon into the evening, around 16:00 to 20:00. The highest hourly value was 173 µg/m³ at 17:00 on 15 August. I counted 10 August days above the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ and 5 days above the EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³. In July, those counts were 5 and 0, so August was clearly higher for ozone.

Across NO2, I did not see a clear citywide change from July.

## Data quality

I could not use **Tiefgarage City Carré**, **Tiefgarage Domviertel**, **Parkhaus Hotel Ratswaage** or **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** for parking because they reported in fewer than 60% of August hours.

I could use all 18 EV charging stations.

I could not use **PM10** or **PM2.5** at **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** or **West** because the hourly values stayed unchanged from one hour to the next in more than half of the reported hours.
