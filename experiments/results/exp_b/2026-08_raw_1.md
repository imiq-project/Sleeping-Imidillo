# Imidillo report, August 2026

I looked through the August numbers and compared them with July. The clearest shifts were in ozone and in a few parking patterns, while EV charging stayed mixed. I also had to leave out several sensors that were too sparse or too flat to trust.

## Highlights

- In parking, I could use 4 lots. Across those lots, weekdays were quietest at 03:00 and busiest at 12:00.
- **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** was much emptier on weekends at 12:00, averaging 44.2%, than on weekdays, averaging 77.5%. That is a gap of 33.4 percentage points.
- **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was much fuller on weekends at 20:00, averaging 47.5%, than on weekdays at that hour, averaging 17.5%. It was also fuller than in July weekend 20:00, when it averaged 21.0%.
- At **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim**, weekday 12:00 occupancy averaged 37.3% in August, up from 23.3% in July, a rise of 14.0 percentage points.
- Ozone at **West** exceeded the WHO 8-hour guideline on 10 days and the EU 8-hour target on 5 days. In July, those counts were 5 days and 0 days.

## Parking

I could describe **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**. Across these four lots, the weekday pattern was clear: the emptiest hour was 03:00, and the fullest hour was 12:00.

The strongest weekday-weekend contrast was at **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**. At 12:00 it averaged 77.5% on weekdays and 44.2% on weekends. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** showed the same direction, with visibly lower weekend midday values than on weekdays.

**Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** went the other way later in the day. At 20:00 it averaged 47.5% on weekends and 17.5% on weekdays. Compared with July at the same weekend hour, that was fuller by 26.5 percentage points.

I did not see one single month-to-month direction across all reliable parking lots. The clearest August increase versus July was at **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim**, where weekday 12:00 occupancy rose from 23.3% to 37.3%. For the other reliable lots, I did not see a single clear overall shift across comparable hours and day types.

## EV charging

I could use 10 charging stations. The reliable station set showed a broad daily rhythm rather than one strong spike: the quietest hour was around 04:00, and the fullest period ran from late morning into early evening, rather than one clearly busiest single hour.

Use usually rose after 08:00, stayed highest through much of 12:00 to 18:00, and then eased later in the evening. I did not see a clear citywide weekend-versus-weekday split across the reliable stations. I also did not see a measurable citywide change from July.

A few stations stood out in shape rather than in direction. **Am Blauen Bock 3** had repeated full hours, **Ernst-Reuter-Allee 6** was active through many daytime and evening hours, and **Hegelstraße 4** stayed busy for long stretches on several days.

## Air quality

For air, I could use all 4 NO2 sensors and the O3 sensor at **West**. I did not use the particle sensors this month because their traces were too flat to trust.

NO2 stayed well below the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³ everywhere. The highest NO2 hour I saw was 98 µg/m³ at **City Tunnel** on 14 August at 21:00. On weekdays, NO2 was usually lowest before 06:00 and higher later in the morning; at **City Tunnel** I also saw a strong evening rise on several days.

The WHO daily-mean NO2 guideline of 25 µg/m³ was exceeded on many days at **City Tunnel**, on fewer days at **Guericke Strasse** and **Schleinufer**, and I did not see a daily exceedance at **West**.

Ozone at **West** followed the opposite daily pattern: lower in the morning and highest from late afternoon into the evening. The monthly peak was 173 µg/m³ on 15 August at 17:00.

The clearest month-to-month air change was ozone. The WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 µg/m³ was exceeded on 10 days in August, up from 5 days in July. The EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ was exceeded on 5 days in August, after 0 days in July.

## Data quality

I could not use these parking sensors because they had readings in fewer than 60% of the August hours: **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Tiefgarage City Carré**, **Tiefgarage Domviertel**, **Parkhaus Hotel Ratswaage** and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**.

I could not use these EV charging stations: **Gareisstraße 12** and **Gustav-Adolf-Straße 31** had readings in fewer than 60% of the hours; **An der Buckauer Fähre 1**, **Bethanienstraße 1**, **Breitscheidstraße 2**, **Halberstädter Strasse 137**, **Julius-Bremer-Straße 1** and **Leipziger Straße 45** changed too little from one hour to the next.

I could not use **PM10** at **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** or **West**, or **PM2.5** at those same three sites, because the values changed too little from one hour to the next.
