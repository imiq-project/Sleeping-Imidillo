# Imidillo report, September 2026

I had a thinner September than usual because many streams went missing from 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00. Outside that gap, I could still see clear patterns in parking, charging and the usable air-quality sensors. I am keeping this report to the sensors that were reliable enough to trust.

## Highlights

- I could use 4 parking lots and 16 EV charging stations reliably this month.
- The clearest parking split was between weekday-heavy **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** and weekend-heavy **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**.
- Across the EV network, the weekday pattern stayed familiar: quietest around 03:00, busiest around 11:00, with no clear month-to-month change from August.
- NO2 stayed well below the EU hourly limit everywhere. The highest value I saw was 118 at **City Tunnel** on 28 September at 18:00.
- Ozone at **West** did not exceed the EU 8-hour target of 120, but the WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 was exceeded on 27 September and 30 September.

## Parking

I could use **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**.

On weekdays, **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** showed the clearest workday shape. It was quietest around 03:00 to 05:00, usually close to 34%, and busiest around 11:00, often around 78% to 86%. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** followed a similar shape: very low around 03:00 to 05:00 at 4.4% to 7.0%, then fullest in late morning, often between about 58% and 83.3%.

Weekend behaviour was very different between lots. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** was usually less full on weekend late mornings than on weekdays: many weekday 10:00 to 12:00 values were in the mid-70s to mid-80s, while Saturday 19 September reached only 45.8% at 10:00 and 49.6% at 12:00, and Sunday 20 September stayed around 36.5% to 38.2% from 11:00 to 13:00. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** showed the opposite pattern. Many weekday late mornings sat roughly between 28% and 52%, but it reached 97.8% on Saturday 19 September at 11:00 and 98.6% on Sunday 20 September at 11:00.

Compared with August, I did not see a clear month-to-month change at **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** or **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**. Their weekday shapes stayed broadly the same. **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** changed the most inside September itself: it was still at 88.0% on 9 September at 11:00, but dropped to 0.0% on 20 September at 11:00 and then stayed mostly below 30% for several days before rising again late in the month.

## EV charging

I could use 16 stations reliably this month.

Across those stations, the general weekday shape was steady. Occupancy was lowest around 03:00, rose from the morning onward, and was busiest around 11:00. After about 20:00, usage eased again. Weekends were flatter, with more mixed daytime use and some stations carrying occupancy further into the night.

A few stations stood out. **Hegelstraße 4** was occupied for long stretches, often at 50% or 100%, including many night hours. **Breiter Weg 203** was often full in daytime. **Liebknechtstraße 37** also had many very busy weekday daytime and evening hours, including repeated 100% readings.

Against August, I did not see a clear overall change in the network at comparable weekday and weekend hours. The daily shape looked much the same.

## Air quality

I could use all 4 NO2 sensors and the **West** O3 sensor.

For NO2, the typical weekday pattern was lowest around 04:00 to 05:00 and highest around 08:00 to 10:00, with another rise later in the day at the roadside sites. Weekends were generally less polluted than weekdays, especially in the morning. At **Schleinufer**, many weekday 07:00 to 09:00 values were around 30 to 45, while several weekend mornings stayed in single digits or the low teens.

I did not see any NO2 breach of the EU hourly limit of 200. The highest hourly value was 118 at **City Tunnel** on 28 September at 18:00. For the WHO daily-mean guideline of 25, I saw exceedances on several days at **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse** and **Schleinufer**. At **West**, I did not see a daily mean above 25.

For ozone at **West**, the highest hourly value was 120 on 28 September at 18:00. The EU 8-hour target of 120 was not exceeded. The WHO 8-hour guideline of 100 was exceeded on 27 September and 30 September. Compared with August, high-ozone periods were less frequent: August had several 8-hour periods above 120, while September had none.

I could not give a reliable PM10 or PM2.5 picture this month.

## Data quality

There was a broad gap from 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00 across parking, EV charging and air-quality streams.

For parking, I could not use **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Tiefgarage City Carré**, **Tiefgarage Domviertel**, **Parkhaus Hotel Ratswaage** or **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** because they had readings in fewer than 60% of the hours.

For EV charging, I used 16 stations. I left out **An der Buckauer Fähre 1** and **Gareisstraße 12** because they had readings in fewer than 60% of the hours.

For air quality, I could use NO2 and O3. I could not use **PM10 Guericke Strasse**, **PM10 Schleinufer**, **PM10 West**, **PM2.5 Guericke Strasse**, **PM2.5 Schleinufer** or **PM2.5 West** because their values stayed unchanged from one hour to the next in more than half of the hours.
