# Imidillo report, September 2026

September was readable, but not clean. From 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00, most of the city’s sensor streams went quiet, so I stayed cautious and only described the sensors that gave me enough trustworthy hours.

Even with that gap, I could still see the month’s main patterns in parking, charging and air quality.

## Highlights

- Most sensor streams had a clear mid-month gap from 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00.
- In parking, the clearest weekday pattern stayed at **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** and **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**: quietest in the small hours and busiest in late morning.
- **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** stood out as the most weekend-led lot, reaching 97.8% at 11:00 on 19 September and 98.6% at 11:00 on 20 September.
- I could use 16 EV charging stations. Across them, charging use was usually lowest overnight, rose after 08:00 and stayed strongest from late morning into early evening.
- I saw no breach of the EU hourly **NO2** limit and no breach of the EU 8-hour **O3** target. At **West**, the WHO 8-hour **O3** guideline was exceeded on 27 and 30 September.

## Parking

I could rely on five of the nine lots: **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim**, **Parkhaus Hotel Ratswaage** and **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**.

The clearest weekday rhythm was at **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz**. It was quietest in the small hours, mostly around 33% to 39% at 03:00 to 05:00, then climbed through the morning and was usually fullest around 10:00 to 12:00, often between 77.1% and 86.7%. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** showed a similar shape: low in the late evening and overnight, then strongest around 10:00 to 12:00, with weekday peaks such as 83.3% on 1 September, 82.5% on 10 September and 77.2% on 29 September.

Weekends looked different. **Tiefgarage Universitätsplatz** was clearly less full on weekend middays than on weekdays. On Sundays, it was around 36.5% to 39.9% from 10:00 to 13:00 on 20 and 27 September, while weekday late-morning values were much higher. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** also ran lower on Sundays: on 20 September it was just 1.3% at 11:00 and 8.8% at 12:00.

The exception was **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**. Its weekday pattern was irregular, but its weekend peaks were unmistakable. It reached 97.8% at 11:00 on Saturday 19 September, 98.6% at 11:00 on Sunday 20 September, and 81.1% at 13:00 on Saturday 26 September.

Compared with August, I did not see a clear citywide shift in parking at comparable days and hours. The weekday late-morning peak at **Universitätsplatz** and **Friedensplatz** remained, and **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** remained the lot with the strongest weekend surges.

## EV charging

I could use 16 charging stations this month.

Across those stations, the typical shape was broad rather than sharp. Occupancy was usually lowest overnight, started to rise after 08:00, and was strongest from about 10:00 to 18:00. Weekends were flatter, with less of the weekday morning ramp and more spread into the afternoon and evening. Long runs at 50% or 100% were common at some stations, and that fits these reconstructed charging states.

Against August, I did not see a clear citywide change in charging use. Some stations were fuller at some hours and others were quieter, but the overall daily shape stayed much the same.

## Air quality

I could rely on **NO2** at **City Tunnel**, **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** and **West**, and on **O3** at **West**.

The **NO2** pattern on weekdays was the usual road-side one: lowest overnight, then higher in the morning and again from late afternoon into the evening. The highest hourly values I saw were 118 at **City Tunnel**, 73 at **Guericke Strasse**, 89 at **Schleinufer** and 44 at **West**. All of these stayed well below the EU hourly **NO2** limit of 200.

For the WHO daily mean **NO2** guideline of 25, I saw exceedances on some days at **City Tunnel**, and on fewer days at **Guericke Strasse** and **Schleinufer**. I did not see that at **West**.

**O3** at **West** showed the opposite daily shape: lower in the morning and higher in the late afternoon and early evening. The monthly high was 120 at 18:00 on 28 September. I did not see any breach of the EU 8-hour **O3** target of 120. The WHO 8-hour **O3** guideline of 100 was exceeded on 27 and 30 September.

Compared with August, ozone was clearly lower. September peaked at 120, while August reached 173. For **NO2**, I did not see one consistent month-to-month direction across all sites.

## Data quality

Most streams had a shared gap from 11 September 12:00 to 14 September 08:00.

For parking, I could not use **Tiefgarage Allee Center**, **Tiefgarage City Carré**, **Tiefgarage Domviertel** or **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** because they reported in fewer than 60% of hours.

For EV charging, I could not use **An der Buckauer Fähre 1** or **Gareisstraße 12** because they also reported in fewer than 60% of hours.

For air quality, I could use **NO2** and **O3**, but I could not use any **PM10** or **PM2.5** sensor. At **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer** and **West**, those particle values stayed unchanged from one hour to the next in more than half of recorded hours, so they were not reliable enough to describe.
