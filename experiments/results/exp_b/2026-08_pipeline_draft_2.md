# Imidillo report, August 2026

I looked at 1 to 31 August 2026 across parking, EV charging, traffic, and air quality in Magdeburg. The clearest shifts from July were fuller parking at **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim**, less busy charging at **Am Blauen Bock 3**, slower traffic on several streets, and repeated NO2 and O3 limit exceedances. I can see where the numbers moved, but the data cannot tell me why.

## Highlights

- **City Tunnel** exceeded the WHO daily NO2 guideline of 25 µg/m³ on 17 days. The worst daily mean was 54.1 µg/m³.
- **West** exceeded the WHO 8-hour O3 guideline of 100 µg/m³ on 15 days and the EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ on 7 days. The worst 8-hour mean was 151.1 µg/m³.
- **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was fuller than in July by 13.9 percentage points, from 24.2% to 37.9%.
- **Am Blauen Bock 3** was less busy than in July by 22.1 percentage points, from 42.6% to 20.0%.
- **Walther Rathenau Strasse** was slower than in July by 1.7 km/h, from 39.3 km/h to 37.8 km/h.

## Parking

I could use 4 of 9 parking lots. On a typical weekday across the usable lots, occupancy was lowest at 3:00 at 25.4% and highest at 12:00 at 43.5%.

Weekend patterns differed by lot. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was fuller on weekends by 7.8 percentage points. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** were emptier by 13.7 and 15.6 percentage points. **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** showed no measurable weekend change.

Compared with July, only **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** became fuller, by 13.9 percentage points. The 95% interval was 10.9 to 17.0 percentage points. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** showed no measurable change.

## EV charging

I could use 15 of 18 charging stations. On a typical weekday, charging point occupancy was lowest at 4:00 at 8.3% and highest at 11:00 at 30.9%.

The weekend effect was mixed across stations, so there was no single citywide weekend pattern. Some stations were busier on weekends, others were less busy.

Among the comparable stations, one became busier, one became less busy, and eleven showed no measurable change from July. **Breiter Weg 203** became busier by 17.9 percentage points, from 25.6% to 44.6%. **Am Blauen Bock 3** became less busy by 22.1 percentage points, from 42.6% to 20.0%, with a 95% interval of -31.2 to -13.1 percentage points.

The busiest stations by time with no point free were **Breiter Weg 203** at 31.0%, **Hegelstraße 4** at 23.4%, and **Hegelstraße 19** at 17.0%.

## Traffic

I could use 14 of 29 traffic sensors. On a typical weekday, average speed was highest at 5:00 at 48.2 km/h and lowest at 16:00 at 34.5 km/h.

Weekend effects differed between streets. Several streets were faster on weekends, while most others showed no measurable weekend change.

Compared with July, six of the comparable streets were slower and none were faster. Eight showed no measurable change. The most notable drops were on **Wittenberger Strasse**, down 1.9 km/h from 31.7 km/h to 29.8 km/h, **Walther Rathenau Strasse**, down 1.7 km/h from 39.3 km/h to 37.8 km/h, and **Gustav Adolf Strasse**, down 1.6 km/h from 31.2 km/h to 29.7 km/h.

## Air quality

For NO2, I could use all four stations. A typical weekday fell to 10.8 µg/m³ at 4:00 and rose to 25.5 µg/m³ at 9:00. Weekend NO2 was lower at **City Tunnel** by 9.3 µg/m³ and at **Guericke Strasse** by 4.8 µg/m³, while **Schleinufer** and **West** showed no measurable weekend change.

No NO2 station exceeded the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³. The WHO daily guideline of 25 µg/m³ was exceeded on 17 days at **City Tunnel**, 3 days at **Guericke Strasse**, and 3 days at **Schleinufer**. **West** had no exceedance. Compared with July, NO2 was more polluted at **City Tunnel** by 6.8 µg/m³, at **Guericke Strasse** by 4.7 µg/m³, and at **West** by 1.8 µg/m³. **Schleinufer** showed no measurable change.

For O3, I could use **West**. O3 was lowest at 8:00 at 46.6 µg/m³ and highest at 18:00 at 102.1 µg/m³. There was no measurable weekend change. The WHO 8-hour guideline was exceeded on 15 days and the EU 8-hour target on 7 days, with a worst 8-hour mean of 151.1 µg/m³. Compared with July, there was no measurable change.

I could not use any PM10 station. **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer**, and **West** were excluded because the value was unchanged from the previous hour in 61%, 59%, and 71% of hours.

I could not use any PM2.5 station. **Guericke Strasse**, **Schleinufer**, and **West** were excluded because the value was unchanged from the previous hour in 72%, 70%, and 78% of hours.
