# Imidillo report, August 2026

I looked at sensor data for 1 to 31 August 2026 and compared it with July 2026. Parking was mostly steady apart from one fuller garage, EV charging was mixed, several traffic streets were slower, and air quality had clear NO2 and ozone exceedances. The data show the shifts, but not why they happened.

## Highlights

- **City Tunnel** exceeded the WHO daily NO2 guideline of 25 µg/m³ on 17 days. The worst daily mean was 54.1 µg/m³.
- **West** exceeded the WHO 8-hour O3 guideline of 100 µg/m³ on 15 days. The worst 8-hour value was 151.1 µg/m³.
- **West** also exceeded the EU 8-hour O3 target of 120 µg/m³ on 7 days. The worst 8-hour value was 151.1 µg/m³.
- **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** was fuller than in July by 13.9 percentage points at equal hour and weekday. Its raw mean moved from 24.2% to 37.9%.
- **Am Blauen Bock 3** was less busy than in July by 22.1 percentage points at equal hour and weekday. Its raw mean moved from 42.6% to 20.0%.

## Parking

I had usable data from 4 of 9 car parks. On a typical weekday, occupancy across them was lowest at 03:00 at 25.4% and highest at 12:00 at 43.5%.

Weekend behaviour differed by car park. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz** was fuller on weekends by 7.8 percentage points. **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz** and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** were emptier on weekends by 13.7 and 15.6 percentage points. **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** showed no measurable weekend effect.

Compared with July at equal hour and weekday, only **Tiefgarage Hotel Maritim** changed measurably, becoming fuller by 13.9 percentage points. **Elbauenpark - Messeparkplatz**, **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, and **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus** showed no measurable change.

## EV charging

I had usable data from 15 of 18 charging stations. A typical weekday started quiet, reached its lowest point at 04:00 with 8.3%, then climbed through the morning to 30.9% at 11:00. After that it eased, while staying fairly active into the early evening.

Weekend behaviour differed between stations. Some stations were busier on weekends, some were less busy, and several showed no measurable weekend effect.

Compared with July, one comparable station was busier and one was less busy; the other 11 comparable stations showed no measurable change. **Breiter Weg 203** was busier by 17.9 percentage points at equal hour and weekday, with the raw mean moving from 25.6% to 44.6%. It was also the busiest site in August, with no free charging point 31.0% of known time. **Am Blauen Bock 3** was less busy by 22.1 percentage points, from 42.6% to 20.0%. Another busy site was **Hegelstraße 4**, where no charging point was free for 23.4% of known time.

## Traffic

I had usable data from 14 of 29 traffic sensors. On a typical weekday, average speeds were highest at 05:00 at 48.2 km/h and lowest at 16:00 at 34.5 km/h.

Weekend behaviour differed between streets. Some streets were faster on weekends, while most showed no measurable weekend effect.

Compared with July, six streets were slower at equal hour and weekday, none were faster, and eight showed no measurable change. The clearest drop was on **Wittenberger Strasse**, down 1.9 km/h, with the raw mean moving from 31.7 km/h to 29.8 km/h. **Walther Rathenau Strasse** was slower by 1.7 km/h, from 39.3 km/h to 37.8 km/h. **Gustav Adolf Strasse** was slower by 1.6 km/h, from 31.2 km/h to 29.7 km/h.

## Air quality

For NO2, I had usable data from 4 stations. The typical weekday profile was lowest at 04:00 at 10.8 µg/m³ and highest at 09:00 at 25.5 µg/m³. Weekend behaviour differed by station. **City Tunnel** and **Guericke Strasse** were cleaner on weekends by 9.3 and 4.8 µg/m³. **Schleinufer** and **West** showed no measurable weekend effect.

No NO2 station exceeded the EU hourly limit of 200 µg/m³. The WHO daily guideline of 25 µg/m³ was exceeded on 17 days at **City Tunnel**, on 3 days at **Guericke Strasse**, and on 3 days at **Schleinufer**. **West** had no WHO daily exceedance. Compared with July, NO2 was more polluted at **City Tunnel** by 6.8 µg/m³, at **Guericke Strasse** by 4.7 µg/m³, and at **West** by 1.8 µg/m³. **Schleinufer** showed no measurable change.

For O3, I had one usable station, **West**. Its typical day was lowest at 08:00 at 46.6 µg/m³ and highest at 18:00 at 102.1 µg/m³. There was no measurable weekend effect and no measurable change from July; the raw mean was 69.1 µg/m³ in July and 73.4 µg/m³ in August.

At **West**, the WHO 8-hour O3 guideline of 100 µg/m³ was exceeded on 15 days, and the EU 8-hour target of 120 µg/m³ on 7 days. The worst 8-hour value was 151.1 µg/m³.

I could not report PM10 because all three stations were excluded as stale: **Guericke Strasse** was unchanged from the previous hour in 61% of hours, **Schleinufer** in 59%, and **West** in 71%.

I could not report PM2.5 because all three stations were excluded as stale: **Guericke Strasse** was unchanged from the previous hour in 72% of hours, **Schleinufer** in 70%, and **West** in 78%.

## Data quality

- Parking: 4 of 9 sites were usable. **Allee-Center**, **City-Carre**, and **Domviertel** were excluded because coverage was 48.9%, 7.0%, and 7.8%. **Hotel-Ratswaage** and **Universitaetsplatz** had no data in this window and were silent all month. The longest usable gap was 56 h at **Tiefgarage Ulrichshaus**. At **Tiefgarage Friedensplatz**, the observed maximum freeSpots was 114, above totalSpots 90, so I used 114 as the capacity.
- EV charging: 15 of 18 stations were usable. **AnderBuckauerFaehre1**, **Bethanienstrasse1**, and **Breitscheidstrasse2** were excluded because state was known for only 53.9%, 57.4%, and 57.7% of hours. The longest usable gap was 99 h at **Gareisstraße 12**.
- Traffic: 14 of 29 streets were usable. The 15 excluded streets were all below 60% coverage; the weakest were **Hohepfortewall** at 4.0%, **Zschokkestrasse** at 9.4%, and **Muehlenstrasse** at 28.4%. The longest usable gap was 11 h at **Erzbergerstrasse**. I also dropped implausible readings above 150 km/h at several usable streets: 6 at **Erzbergerstrasse**, 6 at **Gareisstrasse**, 6 at **Gustav Adolf Strasse**, 7 at **Hohepfortestrasse**, 6 at **Schleinufer**, and 7 at **Universitaetsplatz**.
- Air quality: NO2 and O3 coverage was 90.5% to 94.5%, and the longest gap was 5 h at **Guericke Strasse**. No air station was silent all month. PM10 and PM2.5 had no usable station because all three particle sensors were stale.
